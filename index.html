About me

Welcome, adventurer! I’m Jacopo, aka Takeshi, and I’m the creator and developer of project-aura. This little personal project of mine was born out of my need to practice writing code and use computer systems to lay the groundwork for what I hope will be my future career! So feel free to look around, but watch out for old Aldas’s vials! He might get angry if he finds out you’ve broken one...

The Story

Darkness looms over a distant realm, but the trail of death continues to spread. Are you ready to avenge your family and save the kingdom of Heidenheim?

Game Mechanics

Project-Aura is a text-based RPG that will allow players to shape the kingdom’s history through multiple choices and events that will influence the course of destiny.

Prologue

Nothing remains of your world but ashes, and as the smell of burnt flesh and wood still lingers in your nostrils, you glance northward before running to hide in the forest to keep “them” from finding you...

System Requirements (1988 Edition)

  • OS: MS-DOS 3.3 or higher/AmigaOS
  • Processor: 80286 @ 12MHz (386 Recommended!)
  • Memory: 640KB RAM
  • Graphics: CGA/EGA Video Card
  • Storage: 1.44MB Floppy Disk Drive
